# Product patterns for Cajitas

Reviewed 2026-10-14. Scope: public, official product documentation for Qapital, Monarch, Finch, Apple, and Google. These are documented interaction patterns, not private application runtime findings. No accounts were accessed, proprietary binaries downloaded, or product assets copied into Cajitas. REA browser captures of three official help articles are documented separately in [rea-evidence.md](rea-evidence.md); they establish public-page observations, not private app behavior.

Cajitas is a Mexican Spanish savings journal: people record money they have set aside elsewhere. It does not hold money, connect bank accounts, execute transfers, or guarantee savings. The translations below are our design proposals, not claims about features already shipped in Cajitas.

## Shipping decisions

1. **Daily habit:** store the local calendar date separately from contributions. One successful daily check-in increments habit progress once; checking in neither deposits nor logs money. Pet levels use these habits rather than account balance.
2. **Useful templates:** emergency cushion, a trip, a course, and a personal target. Pre-fill editable names, icons, and a suggested plan; avoid presenting sample amounts as recommended financial advice.
3. **Goal lifecycle:** edit name, target, deadline, and plan without rewriting past contributions. Archive and restore preserve history. Default reporting scope should be visible; user totals must remain explainable when archived records exist.
4. **Actual versus planned:** actual amounts come only from recorded contributions and corrections. Scheduled amounts are intentions. Projections use declared assumptions and a readable label such as “Si sigues este plan”; they are never bank-verified balances or promised returns.
5. **Reminders:** an opt-in downloadable recurring calendar reminder is feasible without a push server. State that the user must add it to their calendar and that calendar notification settings control alerts. A saved preference alone does not mean a notification will be delivered.
6. **Export:** use UTF-8, quote fields and escape quotes/newlines, emit machine-readable dates and validated numeric amounts, and handle user-controlled goal names and notes as untrusted spreadsheet text. [OWASP's CSV injection guidance](https://community.owasp.org/attacks/CSV_Injection) explains why quoting alone does not neutralize formula-prefixed cells and why no mitigation is universal across all spreadsheet consumers. Verify commas, quotes, accents, line breaks, and formula-like text before shipping an export.

## Limits and product voice

This review verifies documented feature intent, not retention impact, accessibility compliance, rendering behavior on physical phones, push delivery, or actual installation. Product help pages can change, and Monarch describes its Goals 3.0 experience as public beta on the reviewed page. Avoid declaring an application “best” based solely on its own documentation.

Adapt the useful behaviors with original components, icons, illustrations, and copy. Use warm Mexican Spanish (“tu guardadito”, “pasito a pasito”) while keeping financial actions precise: “Registrar lo que ya apartaste” and “Cajitas no mueve tu dinero.” Preserve the ability to create a custom cajita rather than requiring a mascot or a template.
